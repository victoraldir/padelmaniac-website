Built around the real game

Everything that makes padel padel: the cage, the glass, the serve and the rules.

Regulation FIP court

A 20 × 10 m court with stepped glass walls, wire mesh fencing, a net at 88 cm and stadium floodlights.

Real padel rules

Bounce before the glass, legal rebounds off your own wall, first and second serves, lets, no serve volleys, deuce and 7-point tiebreaks.

12 shots, one swipe

Length, speed and curve of your swipe pick the shot: drives, lobs, chiquitas, volleys, smashes, bandejas, víboras and side spin.

Ball-machine training

Drill groundstrokes, volleys, overheads and balls off the glass, with a live readout of every swipe and a checklist of all 12 shots.

Play a friend online New

Create a room, send your friend the 6-character code and play a full match against each other, with reconnects and rematches.

No ads, no accounts
