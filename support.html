Playing

How do the controls work?

Choose a control scheme on the main menu (you can switch it from the pause screen too):

2-Thumb Split: drag on the left half of the screen to move, swipe on the right half to hit.

Auto-Move (1-Thumb): your player moves to the ball on their own; swipe anywhere to hit.

How do I pick a shot?

The swipe decides: a straight swipe up is a drive (longer and faster hits harder), a long slow steep swipe is a lob, a tiny flick is a chiquita, and a curved swipe adds side spin. At the net a short swipe volleys. On a high ball, a fast swipe down smashes, a diagonal swipe down plays a bandeja, and a curved diagonal plays a víbora. Training mode shows a live readout of every swipe.

How do I serve?

Drag left or right to move the target ring inside the diagonal service box, then release to serve. You get a first and a second serve. A serve that clips the net and lands in the box is a let.

Why did I lose the point when I hit the serve?

In padel the receiver must let the serve bounce before hitting it. Volleying the serve gives the point to the server.

Playing a friend online

How do I start an online match?

Tap Play Online → Create Room and send your friend the 6-character code. They tap Play Online, enter the code and tap Join. Once you're connected, the player who created the room taps Start Match.

"Room not found"

Check the code: codes never contain 0, O, 1, I, L or U. A code works once, and expires if nobody joins within 15 minutes. Ask your friend to create a new room.

What happens if my connection drops mid-match?

Both games pause and show a reconnect countdown. If you're back within 30 seconds, the interrupted point is replayed and the score stays as it was. If you don't make it back in time, the match is forfeited to your opponent.

"Online unavailable" on my phone

Online play needs a 64-bit Android device. Matches against the computer and training mode work on every device.

"Your opponent has a different game version"

Both players need the same version of the game. Update from Google Play on both devices and try again.

Can I play a random opponent?

Not yet: online matches are with friends you share a room code with.
